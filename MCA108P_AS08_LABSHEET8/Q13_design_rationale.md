# Design Rationale
## Color
Deep green communicates sustainability and trust. Cream/white backgrounds provide visual breathing room. Gold is reserved for primary actions.

## Typography
Large responsive headings establish hierarchy. Body text uses a clean system sans-serif stack for readability and fast loading.

## Audience
The design targets students, professionals and families who want convenient café reservations and environmentally responsible choices.

## Layout
Mobile-first stacked sections become multi-column grids on wider screens.
