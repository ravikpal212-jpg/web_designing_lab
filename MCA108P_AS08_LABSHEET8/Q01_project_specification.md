# Project Specification – GreenBean Café
## Client
Fictional eco-friendly café serving locally sourced coffee and fresh food.

## Target audience
Students, professionals, families and environmentally conscious customers aged 18–45.

## Goals
- Increase table reservations.
- Present menu and café story.
- Build trust through team/testimonials.
- Provide location and social sharing.

## Wireframe
Header/Nav
→ Hero Carousel
→ Menu Cards
→ Team
→ Testimonials
→ Booking Form
→ Contact/Map
→ Footer/Social Links
